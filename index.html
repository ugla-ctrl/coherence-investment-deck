Summary · The one-line concept

Texas Eclipse took 38,000 people to a field for four minutes of totality.

Coherence brings 2,500 of the ones who stayed for the ceremony back to Austin, every spring, and grows to 10,000 by 2029.

Texas Eclipse was a once-in-a-century date. Coherence is the repeatable version: same producer, same audience, an urban-adjacent ranch instead of a remote one, and a format built around Spring Equinox through Full Moon so it works every year.

The venue · Carson Creek Ranch

Sixty acres on the river. Fifteen minutes from downtown. Zero travel days.

Family-owned since 1851, with an amphitheater stage under the pecans, a ceremony garden by the spring and a grove for vendors, lounges and the Bath House. Five minutes from the airport, so a Friday-to-Monday pass costs guests no vacation days on either side.

  • Proven festival site (Euphoria ran here); permits and neighbors known
  • Room to expand: second stage and larger RV Village in years two and three
  • Downtown hotel inventory replaces the campsite build a remote site needs

Cumulative net result about $1.6M over three years. Attendance doubles each year on word of mouth plus paid acquisition and a second stage; blended price rises 5% a year; sponsorship steps up 75% a year; production scales sub-linearly (1.0 to 1.8 to 3.4) because the site, permits and core crew are already paid for. Marketing and operations scale per guest.

Neither the site nor the content areas are a hard cap. The growth inputs are the only limiter, and they are exposed as inputs in the 3-Year Plan tab of the budget.

Risk controls · How the downside is capped

Three gates before the money is spent.

Gate 1 · Dec 15
500 tickets, $100k sponsor cash
Below 350 tickets or $60k: cut production scale to 0.9, pause hires. Far below: reconsider format or dates.
Gate 2 · Feb 1
1,250 tickets
Lock talent and production balances only against sold inventory.
Gate 3 · Mar 1
2,000 tickets
Final site scale, staffing and marketing spend set to actuals.

Deposits are staged by gate so committed cash never runs ahead of sold tickets. Cancellation and weather insurance carried at 2% of revenue. Sensitivity grid (attendance × price × VIP take-rate) lives in the Pricing Benchmarks tab.
